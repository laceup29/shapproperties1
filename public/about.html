COMPANY OVERVIEW

MORE THAN PROPERTY.

Sharp Properties is a Faisalabad-based company operating across real estate, construction and design. We provide professional solutions focused on quality, reliability and lasting value — from general contracting and design-build to renovation, infrastructure development and full project execution.

Led by CEO Muhammad Wakil, our team brings together experienced professionals across construction, design, project management and safety disciplines. We are committed to maintaining the highest standards in every project we undertake.
