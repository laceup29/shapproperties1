PRIVACY POLICY

How we collect, use and protect your information when you interact with Sharp Properties.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, phone number and any other details you submit through contact forms or when requesting our services.

We may also automatically collect certain non-personal information when you visit our website, including your IP address, browser type, device information and browsing activity. This information helps us improve our website and services.

How We Use Information

We may use the information we collect for purposes including:

  • Responding to your inquiries and service requests
  • Providing and improving our real estate, construction and design services
  • Communicating with you about projects, updates and promotions
  • Analyzing website usage to enhance user experience
  • Complying with legal obligations and protecting our rights

We will not sell, trade or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties without your consent, except as described in this policy.

Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical and physical security measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third parties. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

We may also use third-party analytics tools to help us understand how visitors use our website. These tools may collect and store information as described in their respective privacy policies.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies to enhance your browsing experience. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features of the website may not function properly as a result.
